Cortis

Cortis (styled in all caps as CORTIS, Korean: 코르티스) is a South Korean boy band formed by Bighit Music (a subsidiary of HYBE). They are Bighit's third boy group, following BTS and Tomorrow X Together (TXT). They are represented in the United States by Republic Records.

Overview & Group Name

Debut Date: August 18, 2025, with the single "What You Want".Name Meaning: "Cortis" is derived from six letters taken from the phrase "Color Outside the Lines." It symbolizes the group's commitment to thinking freely and breaking away from society's established norms and rules. Fandom Name: Coer (Korean: 코어), representing the fans who act as the core support and stand together with the group.

Musical Style & Artistry

Known for spearheading a skate-punk-inspired, Y2K-leaning aesthetic and fashion sense that has drawn acclaim from publications like Teen Vogue. Recognized as a "young creator crew" due to the members' heavy hands-on involvement in writing, producing, choreography, and visual concepts

Notable Discography

Color Outside the Lines (Debut EP, released September 8, 2025) GreenGreen (EP, released May 4, 2026) "RedRed" (2026 single that became their most commercially successful song to date, hitting number one on the Circle Digital Chart)

Members

Cortis consists of five members:Martin (Martin Edwards / Park Woo-joo) – Leader, Rapper, Vocalist (Canadian-Korean) James (Chao Yufan) – Main Dancer, Rapper, Vocalist, Face of the Group (from Hong Kong) Juhoon (Kim Ju-hoon) – Vocalist, Rapper, Visual Seonghyeon (Eom Seong-hyeon) – Vocalist, Dancer Keonho (An Keon-ho) – Vocalist, Dancer, Visual, Maknae (youngest)
